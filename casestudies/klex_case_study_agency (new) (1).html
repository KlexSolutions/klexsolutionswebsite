Case Study

How a 12-Person UK Agency Reclaimed 40+ Hours a Week and Opened Capacity for New Clients, Without Hiring

A case study in custom automation for a UK B2B agency.

The Setup

A 12-person UK agency, sourcing highly targeted prospects for clients across property, construction, and industrial sectors. Senior reps wore two hats: strategy (defining who to target and why) and grunt work (manually pulling lists, hunting emails, formatting spreadsheets). The first hat was their craft. The second was burning their week.

The Bottleneck

For every new client campaign, the team's senior reps were spending 2-3 full days before any actual prospecting started: manual LinkedIn searches profile by profile; copy-pasting data into Excel; running lists through email-finder tools one at a time; manually sorting and categorising the output; finally handing the cleaned data over to whoever was running the outreach.

The bottleneck wasn't talent. It was that the entire prospecting craft had to wait for the data drudgery to finish first.

Business impact: the agency was capped on how many new client campaigns it could take on each quarter. Growth meant either hiring more senior reps (expensive, slow, hard to find good ones) or burning out the existing team.

What We Built

A single internal hub that replaced every spreadsheet, copy-paste step, and tool-switching moment in the workflow.

The rep experience: paste a LinkedIn search URL into a custom form, click Go. Behind the scenes, the system scrapes the profiles, runs each one through multiple verified email databases for the best match, and lands the results in the agency's CRM, neatly categorised and ready to pitch. No spreadsheet handover.

The crucial design decision: reps stayed in full control of who to search for and how to position the campaign. The automation only handled the data drudgery, never the strategy, never the messaging.

Built on Make.com, integrating directly with the team's existing CRM. No new tools for reps to learn. Output formatting matched exactly to how the team already operated.

The Results

  • 40+ hours a week of senior rep time reclaimed across the team.
  • 80%+ verified email match rate, better than the team was achieving manually.
  • New client campaign setup time: 2-3 days down to roughly 15 minutes.
  • 3 new clients onboarded in the six months following, without a single new hire.
  • Senior reps spent the reclaimed time on strategy and direct selling, the work they were actually hired to do.

Why It Worked

We didn't try to automate the reps' craft. We automated the drudgery around it.

The team's value to clients was their prospecting strategy, knowing exactly who to target and why, based on years operating in their niches. That insight isn't automatable, and it shouldn't be.

What was automatable: the copy-paste, the format-cleaning, the email-hunting, the duplicate-checking. None of that was where the team's value sat. Reps loved the system because nothing they enjoyed got taken away, only the work they dreaded.

The Build

Six weeks to design, build, and test. One week to onboard the team. ROI hit by month two. Reclaimed-time savings alone covered the build cost.
